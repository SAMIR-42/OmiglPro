Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains how OmiglPro handles information when you use our website and anonymous random text chat service. We designed OmiglPro around minimal data collection, temporary conversations, and privacy by default.

Last updated: September 2026

1. About OmiglPro

OmiglPro is an anonymous random text chat platform that allows people to start conversations with strangers without creating an account for the core chat experience.

Our goal is to provide a simple way to meet someone new without requiring a public profile or unnecessary personal information.

2. Information We May Collect

The information handled by OmiglPro depends on how you use the service. We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and improve the platform.

Anonymous chat usage

When you use the anonymous chat service, you may not need to provide your name, email address, phone number, or social media account.

Temporary technical information may be processed by our servers to establish connections, match users, maintain chat sessions, prevent abuse, and keep the service working.

Account information

If we introduce or provide account-based features, we may collect information required to create and maintain your account, such as an email address, username, authentication information, or other details that you voluntarily provide.

Payment information

If you purchase a paid OmiglPro feature or subscription, payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. OmiglPro does not need to directly store your complete card or banking credentials to process such payments.

Payment providers may process payment information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

3. Chat Messages

OmiglPro is designed around temporary conversations. Messages sent through the random chat experience are not intended to become a permanent public conversation history.

Chat data may exist temporarily in server memory or processing systems while a conversation is active or while necessary technical, safety, or moderation processes are being performed.

We do not design the core service around building a permanent searchable database of your conversations.

Important: Temporary or anonymous does not mean that information can never exist anywhere. Technical systems, security controls, moderation processes, reports, or legal requirements may require limited processing or retention of certain data.

4. Information You Choose to Share

You are responsible for the information you voluntarily share with another person during a conversation.

This may include your name, phone number, email address, home address, social media username, photographs, location, workplace, school, financial information, passwords, or other personal details.

We strongly recommend that you do not share sensitive personal information with strangers.

5. Safety, Moderation, and Abuse Prevention

OmiglPro may use automated systems and other technical measures to detect spam, abuse, malicious activity, prohibited content, attempts to share contact information, or other behavior that could harm users or the platform.

These systems may process message content or related technical information while a chat is active or when necessary for safety and moderation.

Users may also report or block other users. Information associated with a report may be retained for a limited period when necessary to investigate abuse, enforce our rules, protect users, or comply with legal obligations.

6. Technical and Security Information

Like most internet services, OmiglPro may process certain technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

Depending on the service configuration, this may include information such as IP address, browser type, device or connection information, timestamps, error information, session identifiers, and security-related events.

This information may be used for purposes including:

  • Maintaining the service
  • Preventing abuse and spam
  • Detecting suspicious activity
  • Protecting the platform and users
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Improving reliability and performance

7. Cookies and Local Storage

OmiglPro may use cookies, local storage, session storage, or similar browser technologies when necessary for the operation of the website.

These technologies may be used to maintain sessions, remember necessary settings, support authentication, or provide security features.

If analytics, advertising, or other optional technologies are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain their use.

8. How We Use Information

Information handled by OmiglPro may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and operating the chat service
  • Matching users and maintaining chat sessions
  • Protecting users from spam and abuse
  • Investigating reports and security incidents
  • Processing accounts and subscriptions
  • Processing payments through payment providers
  • Fixing technical problems
  • Improving reliability and user experience
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations

We do not need your personal identity to provide the basic anonymous chat experience.

9. Information Sharing

OmiglPro does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the platform. For example, hosting, infrastructure, security, authentication, email, analytics, or payment providers may process limited information on our behalf.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, court order, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of OmiglPro, our users, or others.

10. Third-Party Services

Some parts of OmiglPro may rely on third-party services. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Examples may include hosting infrastructure, payment processing, security services, authentication providers, analytics services, or other technical providers.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services when you interact with features that depend on them.

11. Your Conversations Are Not Identity-Proof

OmiglPro does not require you to reveal your identity for the basic random chat experience. However, anonymity on a website is not the same as guaranteed real-world anonymity.

Another person may take screenshots, record their screen, copy messages, or identify themselves to you.

For this reason, do not share information that you would not want another person to know.

12. Data Retention

We aim to keep personal and technical information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

Temporary chat information may be removed when a session ends or when it is no longer required for the operation of the service.

Certain information may need to be retained for longer periods when required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance, or other legitimate operational purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

OmiglPro is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

If you are not legally permitted to use an anonymous random chat service in your jurisdiction, you should not use OmiglPro.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child in circumstances where collection is not permitted, we may take appropriate steps to remove that information.

14. International Users

OmiglPro may be accessed by users from different countries. Depending on where our infrastructure and service providers are located, information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

By using OmiglPro, you acknowledge that internet-based services may involve cross-border processing of technical or account information.

15. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features you use, you may have rights concerning personal information associated with your account or use of the service.

These rights may include requesting access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is processed, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification requirements.

Anonymous chat sessions may not contain enough information to identify a particular user or retrieve a particular conversation after it has ended.

16. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by OmiglPro.

However, no website, server, database, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should also protect your own device, browser, accounts, and passwords where applicable.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, technology, legal requirements, or our data practices change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

Your continued use of OmiglPro after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means that you acknowledge the updated policy.

18. Contact

If you have a privacy-related question, concern, or request regarding OmiglPro, please contact us through the official contact method provided on the OmiglPro website.

We recommend adding a dedicated privacy/contact email here once your official support email is ready.

Privacy by default.

OmiglPro is built around a simple idea: you should be able to start a conversation without first creating a public identity.

Stay careful with what you share, treat other people respectfully, and leave a conversation whenever it no longer feels right.
